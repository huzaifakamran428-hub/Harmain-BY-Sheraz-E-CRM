assets/
=======

logo.jpg
    The business logo (HaramaIn by Sheraz) used in the app sidebar, on the A6
    permit slip header, in the reminder emails, and as the application/tray icon.

    To change it, DO NOT edit this file --
    open the app and go to Settings -> Branding & Bill -> Import Logo.
    That copies your image into the app's own data folder, where it
    overrides this one and survives updates and reinstalls.

    A PNG with a transparent background looks best, because the bill
    header band is dark.

icon.ico / icon.icns  (optional)
    Place an app icon here to have it picked up automatically by the
    PyInstaller build specs in deployment/windows/ and deployment/macos/.
